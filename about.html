Workshops that begin with noticing

The Equal Lens designs age-appropriate workshops for schools, NGOs, and youth spaces. We don’t teach children what to think — we help them observe patterns, ask questions, and reflect.

Our sessions use games, stories, movement, and discussion to surface everyday ideas around gender, fairness, power, and care.

These workshops are designed for children aged 8–14 and can be adapted for classrooms, community spaces, and after-school programs.
