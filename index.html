The Equal Lens

The Equal Lens is an interactive learning initiative designed to help young learners recognize, question, and rethink gender stereotypes through games, group activities, and guided reflection. Instead of telling children what to think, we design experiences that help them notice patterns, ask questions, and build fairness thinking.

Who Is This For

Our programs are designed for early adolescents and youth environments where beliefs are still forming and social norms are actively absorbed.
