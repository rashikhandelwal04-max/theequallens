Learning equality and breaking gender bias through activities, games, and guided workshops for ages 10–12. We don’t teach with lectures — we build awareness through participation, reflection, and play-based learning.

How We Do It

Stereotype Challenge Lab

Students confront common phrases like “boys don’t cry” and rewrite them through group roleplay.

Promise Ball Activity

Each student writes one fairness promise and passes it forward — building shared accountability.

Story Swap Circles

Kids exchange real-life stories and identify hidden gender assumptions together.

Bias Breaker Games

Interactive card & scenario games that expose subtle bias patterns.
