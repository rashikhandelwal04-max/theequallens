What we do

We design immersive workshops for schools and youth spaces that help children notice gender, power, and bias through play, stories, and reflection — without lectures.

Who this is for

Schools. NGOs. Orphanages. Youth programs. Any space where children are learning who they are — long before anyone explains it.
