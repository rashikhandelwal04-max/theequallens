The Equal Lens — About Us

The Equal Lens is a learning initiative designed to help young people recognize, question, and rethink gender stereotypes through games, reflection, and group activities.

  • Bias awareness training
  • Empathy development
  • Fairness thinking
  • Inclusive behavior
  • Game-based learning
  • Group reflection
  • Activity workshops
  • Discussion circles
