What we do

The Equal Lens designs workshops and interactive experiences that help young people notice gender, power, and bias in everyday life — without lectures.

  • Schools & classrooms
  • NGOs & community groups
  • Orphanages & care institutions

We don’t teach equality. We help children notice the world they are already learning from.
